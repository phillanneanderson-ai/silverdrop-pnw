What we do

Glamping-level comfort, without owning a thing.

SilverDrop PNW is a small, local rental service run by Phillip Anderson. You pick the campground, whether that's a state park, a county park, or a private RV park. We deliver a 23-foot Airstream travel trailer, towed by our all-electric Tesla Cybertruck, and have it leveled, hooked up, and ready when you arrive.

Inside you'll find a real bed, a kitchen, a bathroom, and climate control for those cool Pacific Northwest nights. When your stay is over, just walk away. We come back, pack it up, and tow it home.

How it works

Four steps from couch to campfire

  1. 1

    Book

    Pick your dates and reserve a hookup campsite in your name that fits a 24-ft trailer. Send us the confirmation and we'll lock in your trailer.

  2. 2

    We deliver & set up

    On check-in day we tow the trailer to your site, level it, connect power and water, set out the awning and outdoor gear, and give you a quick walkthrough.

  3. 3

    You enjoy

    Paddle, hike, roast s'mores, and sleep in a real bed. The trailer is yours for the whole stay.

  4. 4

    We pick up

    At checkout, just head home. We disconnect, pack up, and tow the trailer away. No cleanup marathon.
